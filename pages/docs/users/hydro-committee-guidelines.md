# Hydro Committee Internal Protocols

**Version 1 - October 2024**

These Internal Protocols are designed to govern the operations and behavior of the Hydro Committee committee members, ensuring that the DAO operates transparently, efficiently, and with integrity as the liquidity platform for the Cosmos ecosystem. This document also includes a Code of Conduct for DAO members to maintain professionalism and accountability in all activities. These protocols will be reviewed and updated as necessary, subject to the approval of DAO members.

## 01. Recitals
Hydro Committee exists to manage the deployment of liquidity across the Cosmos ecosystem through the Hydro platform. The purpose of these Internal Protocols is to establish a framework for committee members' roles, responsibilities, and behaviors, ensuring alignment with the DAO’s mandate of maximizing liquidity efficiency for the benefit of the Cosmos Hub.
Hydro Committee is composed of members responsible for key functions such as liquidity management, whitelist management, auction oversight, export deployment, security monitoring, and ecosystem growth. The protocols established herein will guide Hydro Committee members in executing these functions effectively, with a commitment to transparency and ethical conduct.

## 02. Community Charter
Hydro Committee’s core mandate is to efficiently deploy liquidity within the Cosmos ecosystem through governance and bidding processes on the Hydro platform. The DAO is committed to serving the Cosmos Hub and ATOM holders by optimizing liquidity flows and incentivizing community participation in governance.

All decisions made by Hydro Committee must align with the goal of driving value to ATOM holders, improving governance processes, and enhancing liquidity distribution across the Interchain.

## 03. Mandate and Governance Authority
Hydro Committee acknowledges the Cosmos Hub community as the sovereign voice that governs its operations. Any decisions made by the Hydro Committee, particularly related to governance, resource allocation, and liquidity distribution, are subject to the outcomes of on-chain governance proposals passed by the Cosmos Hub community.
Committee members must adhere to the principle that all on-chain voting outcomes are binding, and any adjustments to the DAO’s mandate, operations, or structure must reflect these outcomes. The community retains the authority to dissolve Hydro Committee, remove personnel, or redirect assets at any time.

## 04. Limited Autonomy
Hydro Committee operates with a limited degree of autonomy, allowing for periodic updates to its Internal Protocols. These updates must be ratified by a majority vote of the DAO committee members. However, any major changes to Hydro Committee’s structure or operations may be overridden by Cosmos Hub governance, as determined by on-chain proposals.

At least once per year, Hydro Committee will review these protocols, adjusting them as necessary to reflect the evolving needs of the DAO and the Cosmos Hub.

## 06. Committee Guidelines
### Roles and Responsibilities:

Each committee member is responsible for their assigned function. They must complete tasks in alignment with the committee’s goals and Hydro’s overall mandate.

### Decision-Making and Voting:
Certain decisions require a committee vote, while others are within the scope of individual roles. Voting is required for:
- Final approval of the list of bidders for a round.
- Expulsion of a bidder for manipulation or bad conduct.
- Expulsion of a committee member for misconduct or violation of the Code of Conduct.

For other routine tasks (e.g., overseeing liquidity performance, whitelist management, security monitoring), committee members have individual decision-making authority without requiring a vote.

### Adding a New Member:
To add a new committee member, a nomination can be submitted by any current member. The process includes:
1. **Nomination:** A current member nominates the candidate.
2. **Vote:** The committee votes on the new member, requiring a majority vote for approval.
3. **Onboarding:** Once approved, the new member is onboarded with defined responsibilities.

### Removing a Member:
If a member fails to meet their responsibilities or violates the Code of Conduct, removal can be initiated:
1. **Grounds for Removal**: Misconduct, corruption, gross negligence, or failure to adhere to responsibilities.
2. **Vote for Removal**: A majority vote is required to remove the member.
3. **Immediate Removal**: Upon successful vote, the member is removed, and their responsibilities are redistributed.

### Handling Conflicts of Interest:
All conflicts of interest must be disclosed immediately. If a member has a potential conflict, they must recuse themselves from any decision-making related to that matter. Members who fail to disclose conflicts may face removal for breach of trust.

## 07. Code of Conduct
Hydro Committee members must adhere to the following standards of behavior:
- **Integrity and Transparency**: All committee members are expected to act with integrity and transparency in all decision-making processes. Any potential conflicts of interest must be disclosed immediately.
- **Collaboration and Respect**: Members must work collaboratively and respectfully, acknowledging that the success of Hydro depends on shared goals and collective decision-making.
- **Commitment to the Cosmos Hub**: The primary focus of the committee is to serve the interests of the Cosmos Hub and its ATOM holders. Any decisions that undermine this mandate will not be tolerated.
- **Zero Tolerance for Corruption**: Any member found to be engaging in corrupt practices, including bribery, misallocation of funds, or misuse of power, will be subject to immediate removal and legal action.


## 08. Conflict Resolution
Disputes within the committee should be resolved through mediation. If mediation fails, the issue can be escalated to Cosmos Hub governance via an on-chain proposal. This ensures transparency and adherence to community-driven decisions.

## 09. Ratification and Amendments
This document will be ratified by a majority vote of Hydro Committee committee members. Any future amendments must follow the same process, ensuring that all members agree to the changes before they become effective.
