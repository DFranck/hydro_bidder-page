# Get started with Hydro

The Hydro User Guide is the main resource for understanding the Hydro platform, a decentralized auction & governance system that allows ATOM staker to vote on which projects receive liquidity. By locking your staked ATOM into Liquid Staking Module (LSM) shares, you can participate in the decision-making process, support projects, and earn financial rewards in the form of tributes.

It is structured to walk you through every step of participating in Hydro, from gaining LSM shares and locking them for voting power to understanding how voting and rewards work. We’ve also included detailed examples to help illustrate how the system functions, ensuring that whether you’re new to the Cosmos ecosystem or a seasoned participant, you’ll be able to get up to speed quickly.

In the following sections, you’ll learn:

-   How to gain LSM shares by staking ATOM.
-   The mechanics of locking LSM shares for voting power and the rules around voting.
-   How to vote for projects, the role of tributes, and how rewards are distributed.
-   Key information about the Hydro launch and how to stay actively involved.

Let’s dive in and explore how you can contribute to the success of the Interchain while earning rewards through Hydro!
