# Welcome!

The Hydro User Guide is the main resource for the Hydro platform, an auction platform for Protocol-Owned-Liquidity.

As we get ready for Hydro's launch, we are initiating a series of Pilot Rounds to make sure that everything is working as expected before re-deploying the complete PoL available to Hydro.

The Pilot Round aims to test all critical features of Hydro, including the voting mechanism, project bidding process, and liquidity deployment. The Pilot Round's smaller scale also gives us a better sense of the bidding dynamics. More generally, it’s an opportunity to fully test the processes, from project setup to liquidity deployment, covering the experience of bidders, voters, and the Hydro committee. 

This document below outlines the elements and processes specific to the Pilot Rounds, which will be removed when Hydro the standard rounds start. 

The public repo for the Hydro platform is on GitHub [here](https://github.com/informalsystems/hydro).

## Pilot rounds

### Committee oversight
During the pilot rounds, the Committee will oversee the allocation & deployment processes. It will also be empowered to intervene directly to mitigate potential risks (smart contract, defi-associated risk, etc.) This may include limiting the allocation of any specific bidder if the project deviates too far away from the committee security guidelines.

### Tribute refunds
At the end of each round, all votes are tallied, and the projects that earn at least one vote are selected to receive liquidity from Hydro. The committee then initiates a series of smart contract messages to process the liquidity exports for the winners. 

This manual oversight ensures that the liquidity is properly and securely deployed, but it may lead to a large number of small, time-consuming deployments.

During pilot rounds, the committee will be granted the authority to arbitrarily set a top 'N' number, not export the liquidity, and upgrade the contract to refund the tributes of the projects that did not rank within the top N.

### Bid submissions
During Pilot Rounds, the bid submission process is simplified to ensure that the bid format and structure are consistent across all submissions. The information submitted by projects will be curated to make the Hydro app more visually appealing to users. The approval process should also be faster & more efficient for projects. The process of project onboarding and bid approval will be with the following steps:
1. Projects submit their bid details via GitHub
2. The Hydro Committee reviews the bid details and leaves a review
3. The Hydro Committee approves or rejects the bid
4. Approved bids get submitted to the Hydro contract from a whitelisted address
5. Once the bid is on-chain, projects can start adding tributes to their bid

### Voting Power
During the pilot round, users will only be able to lock their staked ATOM for the duration of one round, i.e., one month. This minimizes the risks associated with users getting locked for several months as Hydro iterates quickly. As the protocol becomes more stable and progresses toward regular rounds, users will gain the ability to choose longer lock periods, granting them more voting power relative to the amount of staked ATOM. 

### Tranches
A tranche is a sub-pool of liquidity within a liquidity bucket. An example is a dedicated ICS tranche (reserved for ICS projects) within the ATOM liquidity bucket. 

There will not be any tranche during the Pilot Round. This will simplify the flow for users and reduce the workload of the Hydro committee, which is currently operating on a volunteer basis.
