# Welcome!

This User Guide is the primary resource for Hydro, an auction platform for Protocol-Owned-Liquidity built for the Cosmos Hub.

As we prepare for Hydro's full launch, we are initiating a series of Pilot Rounds to ensure that everything is working as expected before re-deploying the complete PoL available to Hydro. The Pilot Rounds aim to test all critical features of Hydro, including the voting mechanism, project bidding process, and liquidity deployment. The smaller scale of the Pilot Round also provides a better understanding of the bidding dynamics More generally, it’s an opportunity to fully test the processes, from project setup to liquidity deployment, including the experience of bidders, voters, and the Hydro committee. 

The document below outlines the elements and processes specific to the Pilot Rounds, which will be removed when Hydro's standard rounds start. 

## Pilot rounds

### Global & Wallet Caps
The first Hydro pilot round will start with two conservative caps:
- A global Hydro cap of 20,000 ATOM locked
- A per-wallet cap of 200 ATOM locked

Caps will be lifted gradually throughout the pilot rounds and removed completely when regular rounds start. 

### POL Cap 
Per [Prop 955](https://www.mintscan.io/cosmos/proposals/955), the total liquidity rerouted to Hydro is expected to be close to 2.5M ATOM. This does not count the protocol-owned rewards generated during the POL deployments.

The Hydro committee [will soon get custody of these funds](https://forum.cosmos.network/t/atom-wars-hydro-pilot-rounds/14626#p-33608-clawbacks-status-7). However, to increase confidence in the system, pilot rounds will start with a conservative amount of 200K native ATOM. More liquidity will be added gradually throughout the pilot rounds and beyond. 

### Committee oversight
During the pilot rounds, the Committee will oversee the allocation and deployment processes. It will also be empowered to intervene directly to mitigate potential risks (smart contract, defi-associated risk, etc.). This may include limiting the allocation of any specific bidder if the project significantly deviates from the committee's [security guidelines](https://hydro.cosmos.network/docs/committee/hydro-guidelines#security-guidelines).

### Tribute refunds
Hydro’s smart contract has a feature to avoid exporting small winnings, which can be very time-consuming in the absence of the Valence integration (each deployment requires the Hydro committee to coordinate and sign a series of messages).

During the first pilot round, the contract is set to refund tributes to the projects that receive less than 5% of the votes. Since the liquidity bucket starts with 200K ATOM, and ATOM is currently ~5 USD, this will roughly correspond to a minimum of $50,000 USD for exports


### Bid submissions
During Pilot Rounds, the bid submission process is simplified to ensure that the bid format and structure are consistent across all submissions. The information submitted by projects will be curated to make the Hydro app more visually appealing to users. The approval process should also be faster & more efficient for projects. The process of project onboarding and bid approval will be with the following steps:
1. Projects submit their bid details via GitHub
2. The Hydro Committee reviews the bid details and leaves a review
3. The Hydro Committee approves or rejects the bid
4. Approved bids get submitted to the Hydro contract from a whitelisted address
5. Once the bid is on-chain, projects can start adding tributes to their bid

### Voting Power
During the pilot rounds, users can only lock their staked ATOM for one round, i.e., one month. This minimizes the risks associated with users getting locked for several months as Hydro iterates quickly. As the protocol becomes more stable and progresses toward regular rounds, users will gain the ability to choose extended lock periods, granting them more voting power relative to the amount of staked ATOM. 

### Tranches
A tranche is a sub-pool of liquidity within a liquidity bucket. An example is a dedicated ICS tranche (reserved for ICS projects) within the ATOM liquidity bucket. 

There will not be any tranche during the Pilot Round. This will simplify the flow for users and reduce the workload of the Hydro committee, which is currently operating on a volunteer basis.
