# Locking LSM Shares

## What are the Voting Rules?

When you lock your LSM shares into Hydro, you gain voting power, which enables you to vote on liquidity distribution proposals. The amount of voting power you receive depends on two factors:

1. Amount of LSM shares locked.
2. Duration of lock-up. (1 month, 3 months, 6 months, 12 months)

The longer the lock-up period, the more voting power you will receive. However, voting power diminishes over time as the lock-up period gets closer to expiration.

### Voting Power Key Points:

- Locking for longer periods (e.g., 12 months) grants more voting power than shorter periods.
- As lock-ups age, voting power decreases, requiring users to refresh or extend their lock-up to maintain influence.

## Multiple Example Scenarios:

### Scenario 1: Short Lock-Up

- A user locks 1000 LSM shares for 1 month.
- They receive 1000 voting power.
- By the end of the one month, their voting power has diminished, and they can either refresh or unlock their shares.

### Scenario 2: Long Lock-Up

- A user locks 5000 LSM shares for 12 months.
- They receive 20,000 voting power.
- Over time, their voting power gradually diminishes but remains significantly higher than shorter lock-ups due to the longer duration.

### Scenario 3: Multiple Lock-Ups

- A user has three lock-ups: 
  - 500 LSM shares locked for 1 month
  - 1500 locked for 6 months
  - 2000 locked for 12 months
- Each lock-up provides different voting power; the user can monitor their combined voting power on the Hydro dashboard.
